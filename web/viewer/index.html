aleArrhythmia / Anatomy viewer

Anatomy viewer

Research use only. This is a research tool, not a medical device, and nothing here should guide a procedure.

No canonical reference space has been chosen. Every coordinate shown belongs to the frame of the geometry on screen and transfers to nothing else.

The default heart is a population mean, the average shape of a cohort of twenty adults, and is not any individual's anatomy.

Nothing shown encodes a measurement or a statistical result. Colours tell structures apart and nothing more.
